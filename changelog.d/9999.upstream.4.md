- **Behaviour change: alpha in svg color masks (upstream #3542)** — an `svg::Style::color` with an alpha below 1 now makes the SVG translucent on wgpu and tiny_skia. Unlike upstream, the mask alpha scales the pixel coverage instead of replacing it, so anti-aliased edges are kept and an opaque mask looks as before. Signex: icons coloured with a translucent mask (chevrons, active bar) render translucent on both renderers, without losing smooth edges.
