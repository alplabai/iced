- **Behaviour change: alpha in wgpu svg color masks (upstream #3542)** — an `svg::Style::color` with an alpha below 1 now makes the SVG translucent on wgpu instead of opaque; visible pixels take the mask's alpha. Signex: dimmed or disabled icons coloured with a translucent mask now render translucent on the GPU renderer; tiny_skia still ignores the mask alpha.
