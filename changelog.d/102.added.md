- **`VirtualList` works inside `scrollable` (#32)** — `scrollable(virtual_list(..)).id(id)` now gives a scrollbar, scroller drag, rail, smooth scrolling, anchors and `reveal` while still building only the visible rows. A list laid out with an unbounded height (an infinite maximum, or the `infinite` flag a vertical scrollable sets) is now as tall as all its rows instead of 4096 px, and scroll operations and `reveal_row` must target the scrollable's `Id`. A list with a bounded height scrolls itself as before. Signex: the library browser can drop its hand-rolled window and `size_sensor` once it adopts this.
