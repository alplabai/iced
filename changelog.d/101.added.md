- **`VirtualList` works inside `scrollable` (#32)** — `scrollable(virtual_list(..)).id(id)` now gives a scrollbar, scroller drag, rail, smooth scrolling, anchors and `reveal` while still building only the visible rows. **Behaviour change:** a list inside a scrollable (the `infinite` limits flag) is now as tall as all its rows and no longer scrolls itself, so scroll operations and `reveal_row` on the list's own `Id` do nothing there and must target the scrollable's `Id`; overlays of rows are now filtered against the visible part of the list. A list with a bounded height, or with a truly infinite maximum (still 4096 px), scrolls itself as before. Signex: the library browser can drop its hand-rolled window and `size_sensor` once it adopts this.
