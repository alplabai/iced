- **Primary clipboard on Linux (#101, upstream #3325)** — adds `ClipboardKind::{Standard, Primary}`, `clipboard::{read_primary, write_primary}` and `Shell::{read,write}_clipboard_primary`, and wires `text_input` and `text_editor` to it. **Behaviour change:** on Linux X11 and Wayland, selecting text with the pointer in a `text_input` or `text_editor` replaces the primary selection, and a middle click focuses the field, moves the caret to the click and pastes the primary selection. Nothing changes on other platforms (primary requests are dropped, never routed to the standard clipboard), secure inputs ignore both, and Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V are unchanged. Signex: its text fields gain middle-click paste and select-to-copy on Linux; macOS and Windows behave as before.
