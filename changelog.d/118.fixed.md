- **vello_cpu draws per-side borders, SVG `currentColor` and canvas geometry in call order (#117)** — the software renderer catches up with what tiny_skia did: `Border::top/right/bottom/left` widths and colours (#3391), `Svg::current_color` with its own cache key (#3434), and canvas shapes, text and images drawn in call order with `Frame::with_clip` child clips applied to shapes (#18). Canvas text past the old glyph atlas limit needs nothing on vello_cpu (#22, test only). Uniform rounded borders now follow the outer curve as on tiny_skia. Behaviour change: canvas shapes now take the layer transformation like text and images, so shapes of a canvas partly clipped by its layer (a scrollable or pane scrolled past the canvas top or left) are drawn in place instead of offset by the clipped amount, shapes and stroke widths follow a scaled layer, and a root frame with a non-zero origin (`Frame::with_bounds`) no longer shifts its shapes, as on wgpu and tiny_skia; child clips used to be dropped entirely. Signex: tab pills with one-sided borders, recoloured SVG icons and symbol canvases (text over or under shapes, clipped children) now match the wgpu output on the software path, and scrolled schematic and symbol canvases render shapes in place, so any workaround for the old offset would now be wrong.
