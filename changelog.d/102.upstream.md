- **Breaking: primary clipboard on Linux and the BSDs (#102, upstream #3325)** — adds `ClipboardKind::{Standard, Primary}`, `clipboard::primary_selection_supported()`, `clipboard::{read_primary, write_primary}` and `Shell::{read,write}_clipboard_primary`, and wires `text_input` and `text_editor` to it. **Breaking:** `core::Clipboard::{reads, write}` now carry the `ClipboardKind` (`Vec<(ClipboardKind, Kind)>`, `Option<(ClipboardKind, Content)>`); `runtime::clipboard::Action::{Read, Write}` gain a `clipboard_kind` field, so a match without `..` stops compiling; `iced_winit::Clipboard::{read, write}` take the `ClipboardKind` first; `core::text::editor::Update` gains `CopyPrimary` and `PastePrimary`. **Behaviour change:** on X11 and Wayland (Linux and the BSDs), selecting text with the pointer in a `text_input` or `text_editor` replaces the primary selection, and a middle click focuses the field, moves the caret to the click and pastes the primary selection (a middle click outside a focused field unfocuses it, like a left click). Secure inputs ignore both, and Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V are unchanged. On macOS, Windows and wasm nothing changes: the widgets do not react to the primary clipboard or the middle button, and primary requests are dropped, never routed to the standard clipboard. Signex: its text fields gain middle-click paste and select-to-copy on Linux; `crates/signex-app/src/task_driver.rs` matches `Action::Read { kind, channel }` and `Action::Write { content, channel }` and must add the new field (and answer `Primary` reads with an error rather than the standard buffer).
