- **`window::set_enabled` and parent centring (#103, #35 slice 2)** — adds `window::set_enabled(id, bool)` (native on Windows, a runtime input filter everywhere; a disabled window keeps drawing and lifecycle events, drops keyboard, mouse, touch, IME and file-drop input, and sees no cursor), focus redirect to the owned dialog, and `window::parent_supported()` (Windows and macOS). Closing the last enabled window enables the remaining ones again. **Behaviour change:** `Position::Centered` with `parent: Some(..)` now centres on the parent window, clamped to its monitor, instead of on the monitor; centring on the monitor now also honours a `scale_factor` other than 1.0. Signex: its modal dialogs can disable the owner and open centred on it; on X11 and Wayland keep an in-window lock, because the filter eats every key on a disabled window.
