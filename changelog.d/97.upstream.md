- **Opaque layers block wheel scrolling; scrollers stay in their track (#97, upstream #3493, #3437)** — `opaque` captured only button presses, so wheel scrolling over a modal or dim layer still reached widgets below it; it now captures wheel scrolling too. With both scrollbars visible, a scroller at its minimum length could run past its track into the corner gap; it is now clamped to the track. **Behaviour change:** wheel events over any `opaque` widget no longer reach layers below, including a `scrollable` that used to scroll through it; the content inside the opaque widget still receives them first, an `opaque` widget inside a `scrollable` now stops the parent scrolling while the cursor is over it, and `event::listen()` subscriptions no longer see wheel events over an `opaque` widget (Signex's three ignore wheel events). Signex: scrolling over the keyboard-shortcuts and about modals and the locked-window overlay no longer scrolls the schematic or panels underneath.
