- **No stretched "zoom" while a window maximizes, restores or resizes on macOS and Windows (#132)** — the wgpu renderer keeps the last frame unscaled at the top-left corner until the frame at the new size is presented: the Metal layer no longer stretches it, and DX12 window swapchains use `DXGI_SCALING_NONE` through the new `alplabai/wgpu` fork (`[patch.crates-io]` on the whole wgpu family).
