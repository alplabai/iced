- **Windows title bar and border colours (#105, #103)** — `window::settings::PlatformSpecific` (Windows) gains `title_background_color`, `title_text_color` and `border_color` (`Option<Color>`, `None` is the system default), and `window::set_title_colors(id, background, text, border)` recolours an open window (each call sets all three, `None` restores the default; Windows 11 only, a no-op elsewhere). The window theme (dark or light) already reaches winit through `set_theme` whenever the program's theme reports a `theme::Mode`. **Breaking:** the Windows `PlatformSpecific` struct loses `Eq` and gains three fields, and `runtime::window::Action` gains `SetTitleColors`, so exhaustive literals and matches stop compiling. Signex: its dialogs and main window frame can follow the theme on Windows.
