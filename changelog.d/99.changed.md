- **Scrollers are at least 20 px long (#99)** — **Behaviour change:** a scroller no longer shrinks to an invisible 2 px with very long content, so it stays visible and grabbable (at most half a very short track); a longer scroller means a shorter free track, so dragging moves the content faster per pixel and rail jump and stop offsets shift. Signex: long lists get a visible, grabbable scroller.
