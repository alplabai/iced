- **Breaking: tooltip API and popover widget (upstream #3544)** — adds the `popover` widget and `overlay::Position` (default `Position::Auto`). `tooltip(content, tip)` drops its `position` argument (set it with `.position(..)`) and its `padding`, `style` and `class` (style the tip widget itself); `Tooltip<W, V>` loses its lifetime and theme parameters. `examples/tooltip` is folded into `tour`. Signex: the house `tooltip` wrapper keeps its three arguments and calls `.position()`, and the 24 `tooltip(..).style(s)` calls become `tooltip(.., container(tip).style(s), ..)`, the container the old tooltip drew, so the look is unchanged.
