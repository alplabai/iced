- **Linux title bar follow-ups (#136, #137, #138, #139)** — on Linux and the BSDs a window that draws its own title bar can be resized from a 6-pixel band along its edges, with the resize cursor; on X11 a right click on a caption region opens the window menu; on Wayland an interactive resize no longer ends with a duplicate `Resized`. The last two through the `alplabai/winit` fork. Signex: the custom title bar resizes from its edges on Linux without its own strips.
